Mom wakes up and makes breakfast as her kids play outside. She told them to come and eat pancakes. They came and happily ate the pancakes.

![Making pancakes](/images/stories/fun-with-kids/breakfast-pancakes.jpg)

Mom was putting water on the plants, and she saw a flower on the strawberry plant. She was so happy because her job is to sell flowers, fruits and tomatoes.

![Gardening together](/images/stories/fun-with-kids/gardening-joy.jpg)

As she sells, her kids run around. They planned to go on a trip. After they got ready, they got into the car and went. They went to a beach, and they had so much fun. Then they went home. After they came home, Mom made dinner and called the kids to eat fried rice. The kids came and ate.

![Enjoying the beach](/images/stories/fun-with-kids/beach-fun.jpg)

In the night, they brushed their teeth and went to bed.
