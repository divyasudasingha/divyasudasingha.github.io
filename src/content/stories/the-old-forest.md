Once upon a time, there was an old forest. It was so old that nobody went there. One day, a girl saw the forest, and she went inside. There she saw a big castle. It was covered with plants and roots.

Gravelly Springs

[Wooshhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh] A witch came out of the window.

“Hello,” said the witch.

“Hi,” said the girl.

“Come in,” said the witch. As they went inside...

The girl and the witch sat down. The girl asked, “Why are you living here alone?” The witch told her that her parents left her here because they could not take care of her, and that she had grown up here. After hearing that, she gave the witch some money and told her that there was a bun shop nearby and she could buy some buns. The witch said, “Thank you.”

“As I told her bye and went home, I can’t stop thinking: how is the witch so kind?” said the girl. After she went upstairs, she lay on her bed and went into a deep sleep.

## Next Day

The girl wakes up and makes breakfast as her kids play outside. She told them to come and eat pancakes. They came and happily ate the pancakes.

The girl was putting water on the plants, and she saw a flower on the strawberry plant. She was so happy because her job is to sell flowers, fruits and tomatoes.

As she sells, her kids run around. They planned to go on a trip. After they got ready, they got into the car and went. They went to a beach, and they had so much fun. Then they went home. After they came home, the girl made dinner and called the kids to eat fried rice. The kids came and ate.

In the night, they brushed their teeth and went to bed.
